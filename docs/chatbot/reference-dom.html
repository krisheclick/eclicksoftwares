About Modi Builders

Modi Builders has been a part of Hyderabad's real estate sector for over 30 years, building homes for thousands of families across the city.

With 25+ completed projects, 12+ active projects and a project value of approximately ₹8,500 crore, we have a strong presence across key locations in Hyderabad, many along the ORR.

As a family-run business, We own our land and maintain direct involvement in our projects, giving us greater control over quality, planning and execution. Our philosophy is simple: Buy What You See. We show you exactly what we build, not just promises through renders and marketing.

From 2 & 3 BHK apartments to duplex and triplex villas, with most apartments under ₹1 crore and villas under ₹2 crore, we offer thoughtfully designed homes with practical layouts, open spaces, landscaped gardens and well-equipped clubhouses.
